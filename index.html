    04 — Sequence Lab · Try it

    sequence, live.

    A tiny working bioinformatics toy — the kind of analysis I do in the lab. Tap bases to build a strand and watch GC content, complement and melting temperature update instantly.

    Tip: GC pairs bond with 3 hydrogen bonds, AT with 2 — that's why GC-rich sequences melt at higher temperatures and make stronger primers. You can also type A, T, G or C on your keyboard — and watch the strand translate into amino acids on the right.
